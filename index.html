I enjoy reverse engineering things, reading The Atlantic and The Economist magazines, and finding connections between technology and world events. Much of my time outside of school is spent understanding geopolitics through the lens of economics.

My summers have been spent working on the bankability group at Black & Veatch, where I conducted feasibility studies on nascent technologies and early-stage firms. By evaluating each company's technologies and financials, I combined my engineering and economics skills to provide actionable growth strategies and risk mitigation plans for our clients.

I find myself at the intersection of technology and the humanities, on the cutting edge of innovation but with enough grounding to know when relentless optimization is necessary and when it is counterproductive.

Despite my love of technology, I have some old-school hobbies: playing the piano, scrupulously cleaning my home, and writing with my fountain pens. I also love whale sharks.
