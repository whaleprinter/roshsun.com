I'm a student at Duke studying Electrical & Computer Engineering and Economics. I'm interested in the systems that make technology work — from transistors and digital logic to computers and the products built around them.

Most of my time is spent building things. I enjoy FPGA development, RTL design, computer architecture, embedded systems, electronics, and 3D printing. I'm particularly interested in hardware that sits close to the boundary between an idea and a physical object.

I like projects that require understanding something all the way down. That might mean writing Verilog for an FPGA, designing a circuit, modifying a 3D printer, or figuring out why something doesn't work and rebuilding it until it does.

This is a small collection of what I'm working on, learning, and building. If you're interested in hardware, engineering, or making interesting things, I'd be happy to hear from you.
